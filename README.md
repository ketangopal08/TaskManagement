# 📝 To-Do Task management

![Project Logo](/landing.jpeg)


**The intuitive and powerful task management application** designed to help you stay organized, take control of your to-do list, and achieve your goals with ease.

## 🚀 Features
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Clean and intuitive design for effortless task tracking.
- **Task Prioritization:** Organize your tasks by priority to focus on what matters most.
- **Reminders & Deadlines:** Never miss a task with customizable reminders and due dates.
- **Categorization:** Group tasks into categories for better organization.
- **Progress Tracking:** Visualize your accomplishments and stay motivated.
- **Mobile-Responsive:** Manage your tasks seamlessly on any device.

- **Backend:** Node.js/Express/Django (update based on your stack)

## 💡 How It Works
1. Add your tasks with descriptions, due dates, and priorities.
2. Categorize and organize your tasks for a clear overview.
3. Track progress as you complete your to-dos and achieve your goals.
